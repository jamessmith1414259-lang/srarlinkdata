PROCEDURE TO RECEIVE YOUR DATA PACKAGE

  1. Select your data package.
  2. Enter your Safaricom number.
  3. Click SEND M-PESA PROMPT.
  4. Wait for the M-Pesa prompt on your phone.
  5. Check the payment amount.
  6. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to authorize payment.
  7. Wait for payment confirmation.
